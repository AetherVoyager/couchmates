# Couchmates: a guide for friends

## Install and sign in

1. Download `couchmates-windows-setup.exe` from the release your host shared and run it. Windows may warn that
   it's unrecognised: choose **More info → Run anyway**.
2. Enter the **Hub address**, **username** and **password** your host sent you.

Updates arrive in the app: when a new version is out, home shows **Update now**.

## Watching

- **The side pane** on the left has Home, Search, My list, Watch together, and the **catalogue**: every catalog your
  host shares, under Movies and Shows. Click the logo or the arrows to fold it to icons. The **refresh** button at
  the bottom reloads everything.
- **Home** opens with a featured title, then rows for **Continue watching**, **My list** and each catalog. Point at
  a row for arrows to scroll it and **Explore all**.
- **A title's page** has Play or Resume (with time left), a trailer, add to My list, mark watched, cast and crew.
  Under the buttons it shows what Play will play: the **version** (when there are several), the video, and the
  file's **audio** and **subtitle** tracks, read from the file before playing. When a file has more than one audio
  track or has subtitles, pick them there; otherwise the player picks by your languages in Settings. If the tracks
  can't be read ahead, they're all in the player's menus. Reading takes a few seconds the first time anyone on your
  Hub opens a file; after that the Hub remembers its tracks, so they show at once for everyone. The app also
  starts reading when you rest the mouse on a movie's poster. Shows have seasons, episodes and a shuffle button.
- **Search** finds any movie or show, not just what's in the library. Titles you can play now say **In library**.
  For anything else, press **Request** to ask your host to add it; if a friend already asked, you're added to their
  request. Shows are asked for one episode at a time: a show's page lists its episodes with a **Request** button
  each, plus buttons for a whole season or every episode. The same goes for titles in the add-on rows on Home
  (such as Cinemeta's popular movies): if one isn't in the library, its page offers **Request** instead of Play.
  A show that is in the library but lacks some episodes lists them under **Episodes missing**, with **Request all
  missing**. Episodes that haven't aired yet and specials can't be requested. With the search box empty, Search lists the requests and their status: *Requested*, *Approved*, *Added* or
  *Declined*, with your host's note. Click one to withdraw yours. When your host answers one of yours, a dot shows on
  **Search** until you look, and when a title you asked for reaches the library the app tells you, with a **Watch**
  button.
- **Catalog pages** have a Back arrow; Esc, Alt+← and the mouse's back button also go back.
- **In the player:** Space pauses, ←/→ skip, ↑/↓ change the volume, M mutes, F goes full screen, N plays the next
  episode, and I shows media info. Audio and subtitle menus are bottom right.

## Watching together

1. Press the people icon and **Start a room**, then send the code (like `K7M-Q2X`) to your friends.
2. Friends press the people icon, enter the code and **Join**.
3. Whatever the host plays opens for everyone. Play, pause and seek are shared.

## Voice chat

You join a room's voice chat muted. Press the mic to talk.

The room sheet has a **Voice check**:
- **Your mic's meter:** it should move when you talk.
- **Each person:** shows *audio reaching you* or *speaking* when their voice arrives, and has a slider for their
  volume.

In **Settings → Voice chat** you can pick your microphone, set everyone's volume, choose how much the movie gets
quieter while people talk, and decide whether to join muted.

### Headsets

- **Wired headsets** (3.5 mm or USB) and **wireless gaming headsets with a USB dongle** give full-quality movie
  sound while you talk.
- **Bluetooth headsets** switch to "hands-free" mode while their mic is in use, so everything, including the movie,
  sounds like a phone call. Either stay muted when you're not talking, or keep the headset for listening and pick
  another microphone in Settings → Voice chat. Newer LE Audio headsets on Windows 11 don't have this problem.

## Your profile

Your avatar (top right) opens **Profile**: change the name others see, see every device you're signed in on and
sign others out, change your password, link Trakt or Simkl, and sign out.

## Something's wrong?

- **"Lists no catalogs" or an empty home:** your host needs to check their Telegram-Stremio settings. Their admin
  guide explains the fix.
- **"This device was signed out":** sign in again. Your host may have reset your password.
- **No voice:** check Settings → Voice chat → Microphone, and ask your host whether voice is set up.
